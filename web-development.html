Questions

Asked before every build

Do you work from Figma? +

Yes, that is the normal handover. Sketch, XD or a Webflow prototype work too. We read the file, flag anything undefined, and agree the gaps before we start.

What if the designs are incomplete? +

Common, and fine. We list what is missing after handover and either design those states ourselves or wait for yours. Either way you see the list before the quote is final.

Do we get the repository? +

Yes. It lives in your GitHub from the first commit, with your hosting and your domains. Nothing is held hostage.

Who hosts it? +

You do. Vercel, Netlify or your own infrastructure. We set it up and hand over the keys.

Can you take over an existing build? +

Usually. We audit it first and tell you honestly whether continuing is cheaper than rebuilding — sometimes it is not.
