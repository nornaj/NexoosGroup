Questions

Asked before every engagement

What minimum ad spend do you recommend? +

We recommend a minimum of $5,000/month in direct ad spend across channels to get statistically meaningful testing data within the first 60 days.

Who owns the ad accounts? +

You do. All campaigns, conversion data, assets and historical performance data remain 100% owned by your company inside your ad accounts.

Do you design the ad creative and landing pages? +

Yes. We write all ad copy, produce graphic and video assets, and build high-converting landing pages tailored specifically to each campaign.

How does tracking work with our CRM? +

We wire UTM parameters and offline conversion tracking into HubSpot, Salesforce, or your CRM so closed-won revenue is automatically attributed to original ad touchpoints.
