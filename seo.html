Questions

Asked before every engagement

How long before we see anything? +

Technical fixes show inside a month. Rankings on terms worth having take two to three quarters. Anyone promising faster is either bidding on your brand name or lying.

Can you guarantee position one? +

No, and nobody can. What we guarantee is the return we agree on. If we miss it, we keep working free of charge.

Do you write the content? +

Yes. Comparison, use-case and solution pages, written by people who read your product docs first. You approve everything before it publishes.

What about links? +

Earned mentions and digital PR only. We do not buy links, and we will not put your domain at risk to move a number faster.

We already have an SEO agency. Can you audit their work? +

Yes. A one-off audit tells you whether the money is working. Sometimes the answer is that your current team is fine and the problem is elsewhere.
